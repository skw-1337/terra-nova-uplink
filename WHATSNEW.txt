TERRA NOVA UPLINK - WHAT'S NEW

2.8 beta 1
- Co-op: your friend's cockpit now matches the game: HUD, map with zoom,
  cockpit buttons and pages, weapon box, compass, STAT and STATUS pages,
  squad list with specialties, all in the game's own font
- Co-op: lock works like the game's: units under the crosshair, coloured IFF
  brackets, lock light, target camera
- Co-op: sound on the guest's side: game sounds from his point of view,
  radio voices, subtitles and music
- Co-op: briefing videos and launch cinematics on both screens
- Co-op: his own infrared, full view (G) and 360 view
- Co-op: one map marker per player, the host's in orange
- Co-op: smoother guest view and lower delay
- Co-op: his own settings: sound and music, subtitles, zoom, crosshair, HUD
  colours, mouse buttons
- Co-op: his shots go where his crosshair points and leave from his own gun,
  with his own weapon and jet sounds
- Co-op: both players pick their gear at the same time
- Co-op: still experimental: for the guest, drone, A.S.F. and guided
  missiles are not there yet
- Co-op: squad orders no longer stall when a friend plays one of your
  squadmates
- Co-op: clear message when Steam is missing (Retry / Exit), and when a
  friend runs another version
- Graphics presets: Original, Classic+, Modern, Light, with a preview of
  every setting
- New installs start in the modern look (old school is one click away)
- See your own legs and feet when you look down, including on slopes
- Footprints for every suit and continuous tracks behind vehicles
- Cloud shadows that follow the clouds, soft edges
- Sky without lines, smoke drawn from the game's own particles
- Impact marks, light from shots, cliffs, sharp stars (each can be turned
  off)
- Ground tiles keep their own edges, no more stray pixels over the view
- Squadmates look right from any angle
- Drone screen fills its panel, "DRONE RECALLED" shown like in the game
- Game: esc opens Uplink's menu: game menu, controls, graphics, options,
  accessibility, quit
- Game: order wheel: hold a button, pick an order, choose who gets it with
  the mouse wheel; "move there" stops in front of walls
- Game: click on the HUD map to place your marker
- Game: gamepad support for solo, host and guest, with remapping
- Game: photo mode on L
- Game: squadmates' subtitles
- Accessibility: colour-blind modes: protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia
- Accessibility: subtitle size: normal, large, larger
- Beta updates: Settings > Updates (off unless you switch it on). Switch it
  off to go back to stable, with your settings as you left them.
- Updates are checked before they replace anything, the version before is
  kept (Tools > Roll back to previous version), and nothing is replaced
  while the game runs.
- Co-op: two players on different versions are told who runs what ("Your
  friend is on Uplink 2.8 beta") instead of a session that breaks.

2.7
- New launcher: Play, Co-op, Maps, In game and Settings each have their page.
- In game: the frame counter is now off unless you switch it on, and you can
  cap the pictures per second (60, 120, 144, 240 or your own number).
- In game: old school look on or off at start, Run instead of Bound, inverted
  mouse and the crosshair are all in one place.
- A destroyed building throws pieces of wall, as in the game, instead of
  yellow dots.
- The view fades to the game's screens and back (end of a mission, Options)
  instead of cutting.
- Fullscreen: the Windows taskbar no longer covers the bottom of the picture,
  also when the size you picked is the size of your screen.
- NVIDIA's overlay is sharp again instead of pixelated.
- Skip the intro videos: a switch on the In game page (off unless you want it).
- Screenshot: Ctrl+F12 saves the picture in Pictures\Terra Nova Uplink.
- Ctrl+Shift+F12 shows or hides the frame counter while you play.
- A new installation starts in Fullscreen.
- Leaving the game by its Exit closes everything, instead of ending on the
  edition's DOS menu ("Press a number key").
- Help: What's new (this page), the log, Report a bug, Discord.

2.6
- The launcher offers its own update again each time you open it.
- Full view (G): the status bars stay in place when you turn with the mouse,
  the HUD keeps following while you are in the air, and the compass letters
  no longer drag a block behind them.

2.5
- Mouse wheel, side buttons and PgUp-type keys work as second binds.
- Crosshair of your choice in look mode: the game's, a dot, or none.
- Shift + move is the game's Bound again; Run without the leap as an option.
- Weapons redrawn from the game's own effects.
- Menus keep their 4:3 shape with sharp pixels.
