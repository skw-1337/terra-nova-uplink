TERRA NOVA UPLINK - WHAT'S NEW

2.7
- New launcher: Play, Co-op, Maps, In game and Settings each have their page.
- In game: the frame counter is now off unless you switch it on, and you can
  cap the pictures per second (60, 120, 144, 240 or your own number).
- In game: old school look on or off at start, Run instead of Bound, inverted
  mouse and the crosshair are all in one place.
- A destroyed building throws pieces of wall, as in the game, instead of
  yellow dots.
- The view fades to the game's screens and back (end of a mission, Options)
  instead of cutting.
- Fullscreen: the Windows taskbar no longer covers the bottom of the picture,
  also when the size you picked is the size of your screen.
- NVIDIA's overlay is sharp again instead of pixelated.
- Skip the intro videos: a switch on the In game page (off unless you want it).
- Screenshot: Ctrl+F12 saves the picture in Pictures\Terra Nova Uplink.
- Ctrl+Shift+F12 shows or hides the frame counter while you play.
- A new installation starts in Fullscreen.
- Leaving the game by its Exit closes everything, instead of ending on the
  edition's DOS menu ("Press a number key").
- Help: What's new (this page), the log, Report a bug, Discord.

2.6
- The launcher offers its own update again each time you open it.
- Full view (G): the status bars stay in place when you turn with the mouse,
  the HUD keeps following while you are in the air, and the compass letters
  no longer drag a block behind them.

2.5
- Mouse wheel, side buttons and PgUp-type keys work as second binds.
- Crosshair of your choice in look mode: the game's, a dot, or none.
- Shift + move is the game's Bound again; Run without the leap as an option.
- Weapons redrawn from the game's own effects.
- Menus keep their 4:3 shape with sharp pixels.
